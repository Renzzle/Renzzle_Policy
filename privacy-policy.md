# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 9, 2026

Studio Isoo, represented by Sang-Min Kang ("we"), handles personal information in the Renzzle app and account (the "Services") as follows.

## 1. What we collect and why

- **Sign-up (required)**: email address, password, nickname
  - Used to sign you in, send verification codes and manage your account.
  - Your nickname is shown to other users in rankings and on puzzles you post.
  - Your password is stored as a one-way hash.
- **Collected automatically**: device identifier, records of how you use the Services
  - Used to prevent duplicate accounts and abuse, and to run the Services and fix problems.
- **Purchases**: store order and transaction details
  - Used to verify purchases, deliver what you bought and handle refunds.
  - Google Play or the App Store processes your payment. We never receive your card details.
- **Support**: your email address and message
  - Used to answer you.

For users in the EEA and UK, our legal bases are:
- performing our contract with you
- legal obligations, for purchase records
- our legitimate interests in security, preventing abuse, and funding the free app with non-personalized ads

## 2. How long we keep it

- We keep account information until you delete your account, and erase it as soon as you do. Puzzles and usage records that remain afterwards can no longer be linked to you.
- The Enforcement Decree of the Korean E-Commerce Act (Article 6) requires us to keep some records for a set period, after which we delete them:
  - purchase records: 5 years
  - records of complaints and disputes (support emails): 3 years
- We delete electronic records so they can't be recovered.

## 3. Service providers and international transfers

- **Oracle (Oracle Cloud Infrastructure)** runs our servers and sends our emails, in the Republic of Korea (Chuncheon).
- **Cloudflare, Inc.** (USA; dpo@cloudflare.com)
  - Carries and protects traffic between the app and our server.
  - Your IP address and requests go through it over the network whenever you use the Services, and it keeps them under its own policies.
  - The Services can't be used without it.
- **Google LLC** (USA; [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy))
  - If you haven't bought ad removal, the ad SDK in the app (AdMob) sends information over the network whenever an ad loads: your IP address, device identifiers such as the advertising ID, and how you interact with the app and ads.
  - Google uses it to show and measure ads and prevent fraud, and keeps it under its own policies. We request non-personalized ads only.
  - To refuse, buy ad removal, or reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
- The transfers to Cloudflare and Google are needed to provide the Services under our contract with you. This policy discloses them as Article 28-8(1)(3) of the Korean Personal Information Protection Act requires.
- We don't share personal information with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

## 4. Your rights

- You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or ask us to stop using it.
- You can change your nickname and password, and delete your account (**Settings → Delete Account**), in the app. For anything else, email renzzle.official@gmail.com from your account's email address.
- We don't ask for your age, and minors may use the Services. A parent or guardian can also make these requests.
- You can complain to a data protection authority, such as Korea's Personal Information Protection Commission or the authority where you live.

## 5. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), store passwords as one-way hashes, and restrict access to our servers and database.

## 6. Cookies

The app doesn't use cookies. You can reset or delete the advertising ID used by the ad SDK in your device settings.

When we change this policy, we post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
